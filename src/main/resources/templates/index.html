About EduMania

EduMania is a modern Learning Management System (LMS) developed to provide a seamless online education experience. Students can enroll in courses, watch video lectures, complete assignments, take quizzes, monitor their progress, and earn certificates after successful completion. Faculty members can create and manage courses, upload educational resources, conduct assessments, and evaluate student performance. Administrators oversee the entire platform by managing users, courses, departments, and ensuring smooth system operations.
